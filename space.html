What is measured?

Nearby-star and galaxy markers use catalog right ascension, declination and distance. Coordinates are not extrapolated to the present day. Distances have measurement uncertainty.

The Milky Way’s arms and individual model stars, galaxy shapes, exoplanet surface colors and black-hole geometry are reconstructions. Marker sizes are enlarged. Stellar colors approximate catalog temperatures where available; they are not observed photographs. No present-day exoplanet orbital phases or axial rotations are claimed.

Deep-space observations reach us after a light-travel delay. This UTC clock is current; these catalogs and telescope images are recorded observations, not a live camera feed.

Data & accuracy

NASA/JPL Horizons body-center positions and velocities. Geometric positions at the displayed instant, without light-travel delay.

NAIF SPICE supplies axial tilt, reference rotation, Earth orientation, and lunar libration. Hyperion’s chaotic orientation is not available.

Five-minute position/velocity samples use cubic Hermite interpolation. One-minute orientation samples use quaternion interpolation. These are ephemeris predictions, not perfectly exact observations.

Surface maps, clouds, gas-giant storms, and night lights are reference imagery, not live photographs. Gas planets and the Sun have reference frames, not a single rigid visible surface.

21 selected moons are included; this is not a catalog of every known moon. Moon-system and close-up views use physical dimensions.

Cinematic overview compresses radial spacing and enlarges bodies to show their surfaces. Live orbital directions, inclinations, and axial orientation are preserved. Real distances keeps proportional positions with enlarged bodies; True scale preserves both sizes and spacing.

Faint full orbit guides are instantaneous two-body ellipses calculated from the loaded position and velocity with JPL's solar gravitational parameter. They illustrate orbital shape, not future JPL predictions. Brighter short arcs are the actual loaded 48-hour trajectories. The solar halo is an illustrative glow, not live corona imagery.
